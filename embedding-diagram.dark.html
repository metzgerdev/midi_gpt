Why summed, not concatenated

Concatenating would work and is the obvious first instinct, but it makes the width a function of how many signals you attach — four would give 512, and every new conditioning track would widen every layer downstream.

Summing holds the width at 128 no matter how many signals arrive, which is the same reason a standard transformer adds its positional embedding rather than pasting it on.

The cost is that rhythm and harmony reach the model as additive offsets on the token's own identity, tangled together in one vector. Disentangling them is left to attention.
